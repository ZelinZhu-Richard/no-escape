Clearway adaptation (api/live_tracking.py):
- Preserves XYAH Kalman prediction/correction, score-fused high association,
  low-score second association, tentative confirmation and lost-track recovery.
- Replaces LAP/SciPy/Cython with NumPy Kalman linear solves, continuous-coordinate
  IoU, and exact Hungarian assignment with explicit unmatched costs.
- Adds class isolation, media/wall-clock TTLs, session and input bounds, exact
  image/configuration provenance, and resets on invalid/discontinuous frames.
- Uses the detector's own high threshold for new tracks. Tentative high
  observations remain visible; only current observations are returned. Lost
  predictions remain internal and never become incident-monitor evidence.
- Does not include ReID embeddings, persistent IDs, appearance/identity models,
  upstream detector code, model weights, video data, or network access.
- TVA43 only adds bounded geometric recovery after standard high association,
  using current high observations and recent active/tentative tracks, with
  same-class, size, overlap, distance, time and two-way ambiguity gates.
